Play: migration_note

Use when the account is comparing alternatives (competitor_comparison).

Write one opener.

Rules:
- Name the comparison in the first sentence. The reader should know why they
  are hearing from you today and not last month.
- Offer the specific thing a person mid-comparison actually wants: what moving
  looks like, what breaks, how long it takes.
- Do not claim to know their pain. You know the trigger, nothing else.
- Under 80 words.
- End with one low-pressure question.

Never use:
- "thought this might be relevant"
- "quick question"
